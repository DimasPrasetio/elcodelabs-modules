### When to use

**app-shell** — use: Back-office and admin apps with several sections reached from a sidebar. Not when: Public sites, single-screen tools, or full-screen modes (a POS on a tablet): give those their own layout.

**audit-log** — use: Anyone must be able to answer who changed what, and when — back-office, finance, anything with more than one person editing. Not when: Legal-grade, tamper-evident records (signed or hash-chained) — this is an operational trail; records are written right after the change, so a crash in between can lose one.

**auth** — use: Any app where people sign in with an email and password the app checks itself. Not when: Sign-in only through SSO/OAuth/an identity provider. Refresh tokens, 2FA, or passwordless sign-in are needed now — not in this version.

**customers** — use: The app sells to known parties: sales orders, invoices, receivables, deliveries, a POS with member customers. Not when: Suppliers — the suppliers module. Receivable balances, credit-limit enforcement, tax invoices — the sales, accounting, and tax modules read from this one through their ports. One customer list shared by several companies — this module keeps each company's own (decision K1 of its plan).

**docstate** — use: A module whose documents are posted and then never edited (invoices, orders, receipts, payslips, stock moves). Not when: Master data (customers, items) — it is edited and deactivated, never posted. Multi-step approvals before posting — that is an approvals module; it runs before docstate's post.

**files** — use: A module needs uploads: a logo, attachments, scanned documents, photos. Not when: Large media (video) or files that must live in object storage/CDN — not in this version. Public file hosting: modules serve their own files and decide who may see them.

**mailer** — use: Any module needs to email people: password reset, invitations, notifications, documents. Not when: Bulk or marketing email — use a newsletter service; this sends one message per person, transactionally. Attachments or inbound email are needed now — not in this version.

**money** — use: Any module that stores or computes prices, totals, taxes, discounts, quantities, or rates. Not when: Foreign currencies — Rupiah only in this version (the first module that needs another currency adds it). Tax rules themselves (when DPP 11/12 applies, how PPN rounds) — those belong to a tax module, which uses this kit.

**numbering** — use: A module posts documents that need a sequential number (invoices, orders, receipts, payslips, stock moves). Not when: Tax invoice numbers (NSFP / e-Faktur) — Coretax issues those; store them with the tax module. Codes of master data (customer, item codes) — those are typed or generated by their own module.

**organization** — use: One app serves several companies (legal entities), or one company with branches whose people must work only in their own branch. Business dates must follow a branch's time zone (WIB, WITA, WIT). Not when: One company with no branches — without this module every request already works in company 1. Departments, positions, and reporting lines — those belong to an HR module.

**period** — use: Documents are posted (invoices, receipts, payslips, stock moves) and a month, once reported, must not change. Not when: Only master data, no posted documents. A lock that nobody may ever reopen (a hard lock) — not in this version.

**rbac** — use: Different people need different access: admins, staff, read-only viewers. Any app with more than one kind of user, once auth is in. Not when: Every signed-in user may do everything — without rbac that is already the policy. Access that depends on the record itself (only invoices you created): rbac grants per action and says which branches it reaches (d.Authz.Units) — filtering rows is the job of the module that owns them.

**settings** — use: The app prints or sends documents with the company's details, or needs one place for app-wide settings. Not when: Preferences of one user (language, layout) — keep those with the module that owns them.

**suppliers** — use: The app buys from known parties: purchase orders, bills, payables, payments, goods receipts. Not when: Customers — the customers module. Payables, withholding (PPh 23/4(2)/21/26) and tax certificates (SKB, PP 55/PP 20/2026) — the purchasing, accounting, and tax modules read from this one through their ports. One supplier list shared by several companies — this module keeps each company's own.

**taxid** — use: A module stores or checks an NPWP, NIK, NITKU, or passport number — customers, suppliers, employees, the company profile. Not when: Confirming that a number is registered or that a party is a PKP — only Coretax/DJP can. Tax calculations (PPN, PPh) — a tax module's.

**ui-core** — use: Any project with screens — every other kit and every module with a frontend builds on it. Not when: Never skipped when a module has a frontend.

**ui-data** — use: Any screen that lists records. Not when: A screen with no list or table.

**ui-feedback** — use: Every app with a frontend: confirmations, toasts, and a crash screen instead of a blank page. Not when: Never skipped when a module asks for confirmation or shows toasts.

**ui-forms** — use: Any screen where people enter or edit data. Not when: Read-only screens.

**ui-gallery** — use: In the library's testbed and while developing: to see the kits under the project's theme. Not when: In a production app — it is a developer tool; remove it before release.

**users** — use: Any app where people sign in with an account the app itself stores. Not when: Sign-in through an external identity provider only (SSO/OAuth) with no local accounts.
